Some people around me, including others working in the same field, were not yet convinced by AI. I could see its possibility. Making that possibility useful meant facing the problems myself, one after another.

The skills and workflows I build come from those encounters. I work through a problem, reflect on what it taught me, seek better methods, and return to the next version. Week after week, that effort becomes HolyMind.
