# A human story — 31 visual beats

The opening woven membrane establishes the approved A material. Twenty-eight narrative beats follow the maker through eight story chapters; professional reflection and invitation close the sequence. Eleven HTML sections remain directly linkable. The professional summary still presents recent experience first.

The dramatic arc is ambition → responsibility → possibility → personal resistance → reflection and repeated effort → synthesis → a useful human experience. The difficult passage uses five beats, less surrounding light, a lowered posture and one persistent amber drawing light. It resolves through returning to the work, not an invented victory or endorsement.

Colour has a stable meaning and is always accompanied by an action, a recognizable object and text. The hue itself is never the only explanation. These scenes are visual metaphors grounded in the owner's account, not reconstructions of specific employer events. No fictional antagonist, failure date, client result or third-party quotation has been added.

## Direction and transitions

- A blueprint lies on a tilted drawing surface, the hand touches the house sketch, and a laptop has a keyboard. Props use opaque dark surfaces with clear contours so the weave behind them does not obscure the action.
- Requirements, connected components, a review document and a broken path each have distinct silhouettes. A book collection, a woven skill and a protective outline distinguish research, synthesis and the developing MOAT.
- The human and hands render in front of the action props. Woven terrain remains behind them; it loses brightness during resistance and regains colour as the work continues.
- Each micro-scene holds its pose before morphing. The environment contracts and releases while the outgoing and incoming objects crossfade. The current action, emotional line, chapter beat and total scene count update together.
- Reverse scrolling reconstructs the same poses, props and colour. The warm hand trail follows the actual trajectory and reverses direction; reduced motion renders static scenes.
- Native HTML lists all 28 narrative actions. No JavaScript is required to read the story, use navigation or open disclosures.

## Editing

Edit `scripts/data/profile-story.json` for actions, captions, poses, props, tone, meaning and energy (0–1). Run `npm run build`. The build synchronizes the generated `config.story` block in `assets/profile/scenes.js`; edit its palette and camera outside that generated block. `geometry.js` owns poses and recognizable props; `living.js` layers them with the weave and light trail.

## Camera direction

Each beat now has a `shot` key. Reusable authored camera presets combine wide isolation, closer inspection, an elevated drafting view, modest opposing side angles and a low-angle moment of persistence. Camera perspective changes smoothly with scrolling; the emotional story and the 31 actions stay intact. See the camera section in `LIVING-PROFILE.md` for exact constraints and the current evidence report.
