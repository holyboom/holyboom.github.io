Release: `living-conviction-v17`. This is a symbolic performance grounded in Boom's account of independent work, uncertainty around AI, direct experience of problems, and weekly refinement. It does not depict a documented incident or add claims about clients, hardship, or results.

The five moments have different jobs:

| Moment | Body and camera | World and carried material | Exit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Isolation | Closed chest, bowed head, cupped hands; the existing rear orbit reveals the maker alone | The ordinary room recedes around one small warm source; the well deepens | The room starts exerting pressure |
| Resistance | A braced reach gives way to a small recoil; a new held hand close-up | Crimson bowed ribs, a split working surface and fragments scattering from its plane | The fractured object stays until the close-up releases |
| Doubt | The palm supports the bowed head; the other hand retains a small remnant; a held portrait | The light remains low, the well deepens and the room quiets | A delayed object/caption handoff makes room for the next decision |
| Resolve | Both hands return to the repair with grounded contact | The same fragment IDs gather; crimson moves continuously toward warm amber | The repaired path can carry the next step |
| Persistence | Supported rise, open chest, the existing light insert and a full-body release | The room opens, the hand-local warm cast broadens, and the world returns | The material and emotional grade release into learning |
