# HolyMind Intent Journey v24

## One question, one destination
The owner approved the five improvements following the v23 review. This release adds a narrative to existing components rather than introducing a new visual theme, new song or new section.

The Workbench has three authored visual demonstrations and four existing steps per scenario. Source artifacts arrange into a connected system, but the amber boundary retains missing/restricted/unproven inputs. An operations draft stops at human review; knowledge includes a reviewable source trail; a venture is an experiment, not a claimed success. The visual is always labeled illustrative. No live AI, retrieval, client evidence, ROI or approval is claimed.

Only explicit challenge/step input or Carry this skill creates a choice. Scrolling cannot change it. The existing carrier can show the choice's skill mark without replacing its geometry. Related gallery entries are annotated, not filtered away. The Contact heading, portal icon and copyable brief reflect the choice. No choice leaves the original generic Contact intact. The brief has a review textarea and clipboard failure fallback. Clear choices restores the generic state.

Storage is versioned and limited to validated challenge/skill IDs and a numeric step, in sessionStorage for the current tab. It survives reload, is resettable, and has no submission, account, tracking or personal-profile inference. Invalid stored IDs are discarded.

## Four sound roles
Piano, Reactions & fireworks, Energy on the line and Atmosphere have separate switches. Optional fine-tune levels attenuate each role from the accepted reference mix; defaults are unity. The old cues-off preference migrates to both piano-off and reaction-off, avoiding unexpected sound. Master mute, gesture unlock and hidden-page lifecycle remain. Piano-synchronous action notes retain current harmony but route to the reaction bus; lowering music cannot unintentionally silence a reaction. Legacy Taps only / Field + taps / Full mix Studio audition remains functional.

All audio assets and score data are unchanged. The piano transport/release/scheduling is retained; the score instrument only adds a separate output route for action notes, using the same sampled keys and room impulse. Firework pan uses its actual crown coordinate within ±0.35. Every source remains audible in mono. No new audio file, generator, oscillator or remote request is added.

## Source-aware light
The same deterministic particle frame supplies gold/cyan/violet light weights and a horizontal light origin. Bounded color is clipped onto the Boom lettering and lightly picked up by its echoes; it clears at both ends and reconstructs during reverse scroll. The brain's existing light location reflects along a narrow orbital rim and its caption. Layers and the final portal retain their source-linked reception. Body copy never flashes. No new time-random flashing function is added, and no formal flash/accessibility certification is claimed.

## Cost and stability
The existing fixed particle model, five alternating crowns, 259 identities, long recorded tails, stable mobile viewport and pin range are preserved. A moving average samples JS drawing cost. A lower glow Canvas DPR can be selected only at the next boundary; no particles, trajectories, animation or sound are removed, and no permanent requestAnimationFrame loop is added. This is not total frame-time instrumentation or a guarantee of phone frame rate.

Updated hints describe sound state and the active scroll/drag method. The release label now reads Intent Journey v24.

## Verification
The suites exercise three-by-four workbench states, explicit skill carry, generic and personalized portals, reloading/clearing invalid state, filtering all 34 skills, actual copy content and a blocked-clipboard fallback, sound preferences and independent gains, action sound with piano gain at zero, reversible material light, native touch and 320–1440 px layouts. Existing viewport-stability, long-tail, score-order and line-gate regression suites are rerun. Baseline audio is compared in OfflineAudioContext, not by marketing claims about perceived loudness.

No physical iPhone speaker, real-device frame rate, flash threshold or complete accessibility audit was performed. The live site and browser-emulated testing are the evidence surface.
