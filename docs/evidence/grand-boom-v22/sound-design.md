# Grand Boom v22


## Grand Boom v22 — larger alternating crowns, recorded bright fireworks

Owner request: much larger fireworks alternating large and small, and authentic high-toned firework sound replacing the disliked synthetic v20 timbre. This supersedes the old tiny radii and synthesized firework bank, not the piano or continuous soundscape.

Five burst identities now alternate large / small / larger / small / largest. Radius weights 1.00, 0.43, 1.18, 0.53, 1.30; 232 fixed-seed particles, longer bright trails and a wider bounded sky above Boom. The signature docking starts earlier to create overhead room without new spacer, pin or native-scroll interference. The existing stable touch viewport dimensions remain the authority.

The complete firework cue lane now uses edited real CC0 recordings from three Freesound authors: Rudmer_Rotteveel, 16FThumaF and cupido-1. The 24 short clips use recorded whistles, impacts and irregular crackling; high-pass/low-pass EQ and bounded transient compression bring out upper detail. Low sine thuds, generated crackle and embedded piano are removed from this lane. Existing Mozart and its reverberation are unchanged.

Source HQ MP3 previews, not original WAVs, are self-hosted with author pages, license notices, exact URLs and hashes. Runtime only fetches a first-party 1,031,304-byte PCM bank plus manifest. It loads without an AudioContext, and events do not synthesize sounds on the scroll frame. Missing samples stay silent rather than playing rejected beeps or replaying a late explosion.

Sound is finite and master-muted, with at most five temporary voices, fade on reversal, no delayed event queue and no new sound preference. Big/small burst output gains differ. Inward cues are artistic re-enveloped reversed recorded excerpts, not physically recorded inward explosions. The motion retraces the exact same geometry, and quiet idle frames are still skipped.

The v21 viewport/pin stabilization, line-only current at +8 dB, all Mozart code/data, all other sound and visual/control modules are byte-checked and preserved. Automated signal/geometry/browser checks are not physical-iPhone listening, performance, flash or hearing-safety certification.

## Measurements
Hann-windowed FFT checks require the new recorded bursts to have a higher energy-weighted spectral centroid than the rejected synthesized burst, and less than 15% energy below 600 Hz. These are design diagnostics, not listening scores. Every clip has finite samples, bounded peak and zero-valued first/last sample.
