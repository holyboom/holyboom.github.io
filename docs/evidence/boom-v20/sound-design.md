# Boom Signature Celebration


## Boom Signature Celebration v20 — approved reversible full-stop moment

The owner approved five HolyMind pixel-neon fireworks originating at the full stop of Boom, scattering above the word and fading; upward scroll reconstructs the same particles, returns the rockets and gathers them back into the dot. This release implements that design rather than another planning document.

- Geometry is a pure function of one scroll progress and a fixed seed. Five distinct burst grammars, 90 primary particles plus 45 glitter pieces. No accumulated velocity, wall-clock random twinkle, new image or stock firework asset. Reverse reuses the same particle identities and trajectories. Pause holds the exact frame.
- The existing signature docking interval is extended to 34% of the viewport height, bounded to 120–300 CSS pixels and the next destination. No new pin, scroll interception or blank spacer. On short landscape screens the arrival marker is earlier so sparks have space above the word. The carrier remains docked for the whole moment, then continues to Contact.
- One viewport canvas, capped DPR, pointer-events none and header clipping. It is drawn only by the existing continuity render clock near the signature; there is no new idle animation loop. Five burst locations adapt to available overhead space. The secondary About heading recedes briefly during the focal moment; it returns at both ends.
- Finite authored charge, launch, burst, glitter, recall, gather, return and seal sounds share the existing AudioContext. Forward bursts are staggered at the actual visual thresholds. Reverse uses independently authored inward textures, not time-reversed piano. Supporting sampled notes are selected from the current Mozart harmony.
- No scroll autoplay unlock. Meaningful prior sound/brain/control gesture is still required, and master mute + existing piano/action cue preference apply. No backlog of skipped sounds; threshold hysteresis, coalescing on fast travel, bounded voices and finite releases prevent chatter. Direction reversal releases old firework tails without changing the piano.
- The +8 dB current bus and its line-only gate are byte-identical to v19. Since the carrier is docked throughout the fireworks, that bus remains silent. Approved ambience, fourteen original brain sounds, written Mozart order, piano natural tails and all other visuals/controls remain unchanged.
- Main-site signature geometry drives the studio iframe too. Its inspection range is deliberately silent navigation, while real scrolling and explicit sound-audition buttons use the production buses. Audio previews are illustrative timed mixes of the production renderer, not physical recordings.
- Automated native wheel/touch, forward/reverse geometry, measured audio, mute, lifecycle, responsive and regression checks are recorded. Physical iPhone sound quality, real-device frame rate, hearing comfort and accessibility/flash certification are not claimed.

## Implementation boundaries

`boom-particles.js` owns immutable geometry, `boom-celebration.js` draws it from the existing continuity frame, and `boom-audio.js` owns finite dedicated celebration sounds. `continuity.js` extends only the signature hold and passes the measured interval/render coordinates. `holymind-audio.js` authorizes/routs the dedicated bus in its existing context, applies existing preferences and disposes it with other audio.

The field, line gate, score, original piano samples and brain sound synthesizer are not edited. Burst notes are chord tones of the active score at the crossing. Pause does not repeat audio, while already playing short tails decay naturally. No actual pyrotechnic recordings, external API or generative-audio service is involved.
