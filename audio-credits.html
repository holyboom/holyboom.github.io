Return to HolyMind

Real piano. Credited craft.

Piano recordings: Alexander Holm — Salamander Grand Piano, recorded from a Yamaha C5 and distributed through the Tone.js audio collection, under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported.

For HolyMind, eleven recorded keys with up to six seconds of natural decay were selected, trimmed, level-adjusted and resampled to a first-party mono PCM bank. Playback uses nearby-note repitching, release envelopes, filtering and short room reflections. The Mozart score follows its written melody and accompaniment, while section-sensitive filtering and resonance keep the HolyMind character. The source author does not endorse this site.

These credits cover the piano samples and derived piano cues. The existing electric brain effects, ambient field and scroll instrument remain source-authored synthesis and are not piano recordings. No generative-audio model is used.

Mozart, arranged for scrolling

Composition: Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, Sonata in C major K.545, first movement. Digital transcription by Alejandro Sierra / Mutopia Project, Mutopia-2013/09/01-998, licensed CC BY-SA 3.0. HolyMind retains the first twelve bars, adds a two-bar closing cadence, softens dynamics and uses a slower scroll-controlled tempo. The derived score, arrangement and exported musical previews are CC BY-SA 3.0; the sample recordings remain CC BY 3.0.

Recorded fireworks

The Grand Boom cues use real CC0 field recordings by Rudmer_Rotteveel, 16FThumaF and cupido-1, distributed on Freesound. Short edits emphasize rocket whistles and bright crackling. No generated noise or piano is mixed into these firework samples; the sonata is separate.
