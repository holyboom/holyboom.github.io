Return to HolyMind

Real piano. Credited craft.

Piano recordings: Alexander Holm — Salamander Grand Piano, recorded from a Yamaha C5 and distributed through the Tone.js audio collection, under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported.

For HolyMind, seven recorded keys were selected, trimmed, level-adjusted and resampled to a first-party mono PCM bank. Playback uses nearby-note repitching, release envelopes, filtering and short room reflections. Original short phrases and subtle synthesized textures were arranged to fit each scene. The source author does not endorse this site.

These credits cover the piano samples and derived piano cues. The existing electric brain effects, ambient field and scroll instrument remain source-authored synthesis and are not piano recordings. No generative-audio model is used.
