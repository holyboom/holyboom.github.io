# Piano sample credits

Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm. Yamaha C5 piano recordings, distributed through Tonejs/audio.

Changes for HolyMind: selected seven pitches; used the left microphone channel; trimmed leading silence and tails; resampled to 32 kHz mono PCM16; adjusted levels; applied release envelopes, nearby-note repitching, low-pass filtering and short room reflections; arranged short original phrases and mixed with original quiet digital/electrical textures. The piano is sampled from real recordings; the textures are source-authored synthesis. No endorsement by the sample author is implied.
