# Grand Boom — recorded firework sources

All source recordings are CC0 1.0 as stated on their Freesound author pages.

- Rudmer_Rotteveel — Whistle and Explosion Single_Firework (336008): https://freesound.org/people/Rudmer_Rotteveel/sounds/336008/
- 16FThumaF — Fireworks Crackle (505264): https://freesound.org/people/16FThumaF/sounds/505264/
- cupido-1 — fireworks whistle.WAV (456316): https://freesound.org/people/cupido-1/sounds/456316/

License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Downloaded representations: the authors’ public HQ MP3 previews, not the original lossless WAVs. Source URLs and SHA256 hashes are in ../grand-boom-v22/source/provenance.json.

HolyMind edits: short excerpts, mono conversion, resampling, high-pass/low-pass EQ, level balancing, modest playback-rate variation, light transient compression and finite fades. Recorded whistle/pop and crackle excerpts are layered for five distinct outward bursts. Inward cues use re-enveloped reversed excerpts; these are an artistic time-reversal effect, not recordings of a physical inward explosion. No synthesized oscillators, AI sound model, piano, or generated noise is present in the firework bank. The existing piano music is a separate unchanged licensed system. No source-author endorsement is implied.


## Afterglow v23 edits
Same three CC0 source recordings and hashes as ../grand-boom-v22/source/provenance.json.
