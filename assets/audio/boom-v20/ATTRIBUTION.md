# Boom Signature Celebration

Firework textures are original parameter-authored synthesis for HolyMind, not recordings of explosions and not generated by a generative-audio model. Supportive piano notes use the existing Alexander Holm / Salamander Grand Piano samples, CC BY 3.0. See ../scroll-score-v17/ATTRIBUTION.md and /audio-credits.html.

Preview files are illustrative timed mixes of the exact production cue renderer at the default gain, before live stereo panning and compression. Real scrolling determines timing and current Mozart harmony. These are not microphone recordings or physical-phone listening tests.
