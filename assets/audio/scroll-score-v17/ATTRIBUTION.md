# Long piano sample bank

Salamander Grand Piano recordings by Alexander Holm, distributed by Tonejs/audio under CC BY 3.0 Unported: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ . Source: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander .

Changes: eleven recorded pitches selected; left microphone channel; leading silence trimmed, resampled to 24 kHz mono PCM16; level-adjusted; six-second natural sample decays retained with a final 800 ms fade. Nearby pitches are transposed at playback, with authored envelopes, filtering and reverberation. Original recording notices and URL/hash provenance are in source/. No endorsement is implied.

These samples are CC BY 3.0; derived Mozart score/arrangement and musical previews use the score attribution and CC BY-SA 3.0 in assets/score/mozart-k545/ATTRIBUTION.md.
