Score, samples and implementation

Digital score: Alejandro Sierra / Mutopia Project, CC BY-SA 3.0. The first twelve complete bars are extracted in score order; HolyMind adds two closing bars and uses a softer, slower interactive performance. Score data, arrangement and musical previews are CC BY-SA 3.0.

Piano recordings: Alexander Holm / Salamander Grand Piano, CC BY 3.0. Eleven sampled keys retain up to six seconds of natural decay. The production instrument adds overlapping note voices, per-note release envelopes, section-sensitive filtering and a restrained room response. Existing HolyMind electric brain effects and Quiet Current remain separate.

Browser checks establish routing, timing and measured audio, not a physical iPhone listening assessment or a guarantee of subjective pleasantness.
