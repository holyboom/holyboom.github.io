The thinking behind the sound

The field uses restrained tonal foundations and seeded, filtered electrical texture. Slow asynchronous modulation gives it movement without a lead melody. Scroll controls one continuous filament: smoothed speed shapes density, direction changes openness, and assembly progress opens its spectrum. A soft, new current-settling cue replaces the four reused tap milestones.

The three buses share one AudioContext. Ambient and scroll yield when a brain reaction occurs; silence and manual controls stay intentional. Background ambience and scroll response can be disabled independently in the showcase’s Sound mix. Muting sound never stops Motion.

Sound requires an explicit tap. A hidden or interrupted page stops its audio; return requires another tap. This prototype does not promise an emotional, therapeutic or engagement outcome. Physical iPhone speaker quality and subjective pleasantness still require listening on the device.
